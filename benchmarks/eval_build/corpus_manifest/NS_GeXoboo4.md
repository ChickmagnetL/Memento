## L3 brief
Jeff Nippard tests a $10 budget versus a $10,000 budget for fitness, concluding that high spending provides luxury and convenience, but low-cost fundamentals are fully sufficient for achieving top results.

## L2 summary
Fitness creator Jeff Nippard conducts a two-day experiment comparing a strict $10 daily budget against an extravagant $10,000 budget to test if expensive protocols yield better fitness gains. On the $10 day, he utilizes budget-friendly staples like oats, milk, chicken breast, frozen vegetables, and basic supplements (creatine and coffee), paired with a low-cost gym membership. On the $10,000 day, he hires a celebrity chef, works out with elite hypertrophy coach Joe Bennett, visits a sensory deprivation tank and luxury spa, and gifts the remaining $4,000 budget to a subscriber. Nippard concludes that while luxury options offer convenience, better flavor, and expert guidance, essential fitness fundamentals—such as hard training, proper protein and calorie intake, walking, and adequate sleep—are either low-cost or free, proving that an expensive budget is not required to build muscle effectively.
