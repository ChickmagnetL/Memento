## L3 brief
Dr. Mike Israetel explains how to recognize when high training volume is hindering muscle growth and outlines a step-by-step "volume reset" strategy to resensitize muscles and optimize long-term progress.

## L2 summary
In this video, Dr. Mike Israetel discusses how to identify if you are performing too much training volume and provides a structured strategy to fix it. Common signs of excessive volume include plateaued performance every few weeks, chronic soreness, poor pumps during workouts, and an inability to progress on lower-rep sets. To resensitize the body and drop accumulated fatigue, he recommends a "volume reset"—a period consisting of an active rest phase followed by two to four weeks of maintenance training at roughly one-third of normal volume. After resetting, trainees should restart training at half their former starting set volume, keeping sets low and only making gradual, single-set increases when progress or recovery clearly demands it. Over multiple mesocycles, this process allows lifters to auto-regulate and find their optimal long-term training volume for maximal hypertrophy with minimal fatigue.
