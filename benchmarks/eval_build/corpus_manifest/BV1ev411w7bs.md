## L2 summary
In this Bilibili video, content creator Kai Shengwang (凯圣王) details a practical guide to muscle-building nutrition and macro ratios. He emphasizes that dirty bulking and excessively low body fat levels are counterproductive, recommending an optimal body fat range of 12%–18% (12%–15% for ectomorphs and 15%–18% for endomorphs) to protect immune function and maintain healthy testosterone levels. For meal planning, he presents both total-calorie target models (a 6:2:2 or 5:2:3 carbohydrate-protein-fat ratio based on 25–35 kcal per kg of target body weight) and bodyweight-based macro calculations (2–3.5g/kg carbs, 1.2–1.5g/kg protein, and 1–1.2g/kg fats). Furthermore, he stresses the importance of all seven nutrient categories, advocating for fast and slow-digesting carbohydrates, frequent moderate protein portions rather than huge single meals, balanced fatty acids, sufficient hydration (2–3.5+ liters per day), and plenty of colorful vegetables for micronutrients and antioxidant support. Finally, he outlines a structured daily timeline, mapping out carbohydrate and protein timing before, during, and after workouts, as well as a pre-bed casein/protein and healthy fat snack to optimize recovery.
