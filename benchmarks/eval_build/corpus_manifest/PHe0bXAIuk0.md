## L3 brief
Ray Dalio explains how the economy works as a machine driven by transactions, productivity growth, and short- and long-term debt cycles.

## L2 summary
Ray Dalio presents a simplified framework explaining that the economy functions like a machine driven by individual transactions and three primary forces: productivity growth, the short-term debt cycle, and the long-term debt cycle. While productivity growth drives long-term living standards, credit expansion drives short-term fluctuations by allowing spending to exceed income, creating predictable cycles of expansion and contraction. When long-term debt accumulates to sustainable limits, economies undergo deleveraging, which requires policy makers to carefully balance deflationary measures (austerity, debt restructuring, and wealth redistribution) with inflationary ones (printing money) to achieve a stable economic recovery. Ultimately, individuals and governments must keep debt growth below income growth and ensure income growth aligns with productivity to avoid severe economic distress.
